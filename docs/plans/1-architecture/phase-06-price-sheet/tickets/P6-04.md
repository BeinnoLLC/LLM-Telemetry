# P6-04 · Job calculator covers every model, with local vs metered made clear

**Phase:** 6 — Price sheet  **Complexity:** 🟢 low  **Estimate:** ⏱ 2h
**Depends:** P6-01
**Status:** not started

## Problem

The "Price a job" calculator populates its `<select>` from the same
traffic-filtered list, so you cannot price a job on a model you have not
already used — which is the main reason to price a job.

Also: local models are costed by electricity and metered models by list price.
The note says so in prose, but the output is a bare dollar figure. Comparing
$0.04 of power against $2.10 of billing as if they were the same unit is
misleading at a glance.

## Change

- Populate the model select from the full universe (P6-01).
- Group the dropdown: local / metered / unpriced.
- Label the result with its kind: "≈ $0.04 electricity" vs "$2.10 billed".
- Unpriced model selected → say "no rate available", not "$0".

## Acceptance

- [ ] Every catalogue model is selectable.

- [ ] Unpriced selection shows "no rate", never $0.
