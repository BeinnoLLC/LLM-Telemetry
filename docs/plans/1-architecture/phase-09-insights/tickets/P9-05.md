# P9-05 · Flag unpriced models that are silently costing $0

**Phase 9 — Brand, transcript preview, and unused-signal panels** · ticket `P9-05` · complexity 🟢 low · est ⏱ 2h · depends: -

## Finding

Cost by model over 1M tokens, straight from the DB:

    claude-sonnet-5      $533.16      3.7M tok
    claude-opus-5        $  0.00      8.7M tok
    glm-5.3-flash        $  0.00     44.4M tok
    qwen3-coder:30b      $  0.00     52.6M tok
    gpt-oss:20b          $  0.00      6.2M tok
    qwen3.8:latest       $  0.00      3.5M tok

`claude-opus-5` burned **8.7M tokens and reports $0.00**. It is a hosted,
metered model — that is not free, it is *unpriced*: no rate resolved, so it
contributes zero and disappears from every cost total.

`glm-5.3-flash` at 44.4M tokens is the same question mark.

The local models (`qwen3-coder`, `gpt-oss`, `qwen3.8`) are separately covered by
Phase 7 — they should show electricity cost, not $0.

## Why it matters

A model with heavy traffic and a $0 cost is indistinguishable from a genuinely
free one. Every cost total on the dashboard is therefore understated by an
unknown amount, and nothing says so.

## Change

- A KPI or banner: "N models with traffic have no resolved price" linking to the
  price sheet, which already flags unpriced rows red.
- In the model table, mark unpriced rows explicitly rather than rendering `$0.00`
  as if it were a measured zero.
- Distinguish three cases that currently all render as `$0.00`:
  **unpriced** (rate missing), **local** (electricity, per Phase 7), and
  **genuinely free** (`:free` tier).

## Acceptance

- [ ] Unpriced-model count computed from the payload, not hardcoded.
- [ ] Unpriced / local / free-tier are visually distinct in the model table.
- [ ] `claude-opus-5` and `glm-5.3-flash` appear as unpriced, not free, on the
      sample data.
- [ ] Zero unpriced models hides the warning entirely.
- [ ] Cost totals state that they exclude unpriced traffic.
